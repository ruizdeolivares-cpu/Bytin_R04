# LA COMUNIDAD DEL 13 · Escenarios en Grok (paso 2)

## Cómo generarlos
- Grok Imagine → **Imagen**, **9:16**, máxima calidad. Genera 4 de cada uno y elige el mejor.
- Pega ESTILO + el bloque del escenario en un único prompt.
- Los escenarios van **vacíos, sin personajes**. Yo meto después a los vecinos encima (con las fichas) y esa imagen es la que animas en Grok. Así las caras no cambian de un clip a otro.
- Si el «13» sale mal escrito, da igual: lo corrijo yo en edición.
- Orden: primero los 5 fijos de la biblia y después los 2 extra (los necesitan los episodios 1, 5 y 6).

## ESTILO (delante de cada prompt)
```
Background art for an adult animated comedy series, 1960s American pop-art comic-book style: thick black ink outlines, flat saturated colors (lemon yellow, hot pink, electric cyan, black), halftone dots, bold shadows, slightly exaggerated cartoon perspective. A typical old Spanish apartment building from the 1970s. EMPTY scene, no people, no characters, no animals. Eye-level camera, clear empty floor space in the foreground and centre for characters to stand. No text except the number 13 where indicated, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 1. ASCENSOR
```
Inside a tiny old Spanish lift. Wood-panelled walls painted lemon yellow, a huge mirror covering the back wall reflecting the empty lift, brass handrail, an old metal button panel with round buttons, a flickering ceiling light, a small cork board on the side wall with empty space for notes. A big brass plaque with the number 13 above the door. Cramped and claustrophobic, seen from the doorway.
```

## 2. PORTAL
```
Entrance hall of an old Spanish apartment building. Black-and-white terrazzo floor, a wall of dented metal mailboxes, a heavy glass-and-wrought-iron front door, a dusty plastic plant in a pot, an empty doorman's desk with an old lamp, the lift door at the back. The number 13 in big hot pink letters on the glass of the front door. Slightly shabby and gossip-ready.
```

## 3. ESCALERA
```
Old Spanish apartment building staircase seen from below, steep steps spiralling upwards, worn wooden handrail, lemon-yellow tiled walls, a timer light switch glowing on the wall, deep black shadows on the upper floors, a dramatic low-angle comic perspective. A small number 13 painted on the wall.
```

## 4. RELLANO
```
Landing of an old Spanish apartment building. Two facing apartment doors in dark wood with peepholes and doormats, a big leafy plant in a pot between them (big enough for someone to hide behind), a hanging ceiling lamp, the lift door to the side, cyan and yellow walls. Quiet, suspicious atmosphere, as if someone is always watching through a peephole.
```

## 5. PISO DE SONIA
```
Inside a mysterious, minimalist Spanish flat. Dark walls, blinds half closed letting in stripes of light, a black armchair, a small table with a vintage teapot and one teacup, a wall covered with old photos and papers connected with red string, cardboard boxes, a single standing lamp. Elegant, secretive and a little creepy.
```

## 6. EXTRA · FACHADA (plano de apertura)
```
Exterior of an old 6-storey Spanish apartment building on a narrow street, balconies with clothes hanging to dry, satellite dishes, air conditioning units, closed shutters, flower pots, one window with someone's silhouette behind the curtain. A big number 13 above the front door. Dramatic low-angle view, lemon-yellow sky with halftone dots.
```

## 7. EXTRA · TRASTERO DE EUSEBIO (ep. 5)
```
A forgotten storage room in a Spanish building basement. Bare concrete walls, a single bare light bulb hanging from the ceiling, an old wooden chair, a dusty lamp, a framed photo of the building on the wall, a dented metal box on a shelf, cobwebs, piles of old boxes. Dark, dusty and mysterious, with deep black shadows and a beam of light from the door.
```

## Versión de noche (episodios 1 y 3, a las 2:00)

```
Same scene, exact same layout, at 2 a.m.: dark blue night lighting, only the ceiling light on, long black shadows, mysterious atmosphere.
```
